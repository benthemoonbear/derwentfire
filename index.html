Does the law apply to you?

The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 replaced over 70 pieces of fire safety law. The Responsible Person must carry out a fire risk assessment and maintain a fire management plan. Failure to do so could result in a fine, imprisonment, or both.

Questions

What is a fire risk assessment?
A careful review of your premises to identify fire hazards, the people at risk, and whether your precautions are adequate: escape routes, fire doors, alarms, signage and procedures. It's documented with an action log of anything that needs improving.
Who is the Responsible Person?
Usually the employer, owner, landlord or whoever has control of the premises. If you provide accommodation for paying guests, let an HMO, or manage a building with communal areas, it is very likely to be you.
What happens if I don't have one?
Under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, failing to carry out a fire risk assessment could result in a fine, imprisonment, or both. It can also affect your insurance.
Can I do it myself?
The assessment must be suitable and sufficient. Many people lack the competence and experience to produce one that satisfies both the law and their insurer, which is why the Government recommends an independently registered assessor.
What will I receive?
A document containing your fire risk assessment, together with an action log.
Which areas do you cover?
We're based in Belper, near Derby, and work throughout Derbyshire, Staffordshire and Nottinghamshire.
Has fire safety law changed recently?
Yes. The Fire Safety Act 2021 confirmed that, in buildings with two or more homes, the Order covers the structure, external walls and flat entrance doors. The Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022 added duties for blocks of flats, and since October 2023 the Building Safety Act 2022 requires every Responsible Person to record the full fire risk assessment in writing, along with who carried it out. We assess against the current rules.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes, we offer discounts for multiple buildings.
